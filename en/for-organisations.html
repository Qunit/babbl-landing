For organisations

Shorten the path to your help.

The village car, the neighbourhood team, the shopping service, the afternoon activity club: there's a lot out there. But the people who need it don't always find their way to it. Babbl can shorten that path — simply through a conversation.

Who it's for

Organisations that support independent living

Babbl is there for the community support network in the neighbourhood: support that is freely available and helps people stay involved.

🤝

Welfare organisations and neighbourhood teams

Making local activities, social get-togethers and help easy to find — for those who don't read the local newsletter every week.

🏛️

Local councils

Bringing community support and support for family carers closer to residents, in plain language.

👥

Older people's associations and clubs

Reaching members with news and activities, and helping them with the things they often have questions about.

🚗

Transport initiatives

Booking a ride in the village car just by asking for it, without a phone call or a form.

🧺

Neighbourhood and village initiatives

Shopping services, meals, odd jobs: making the volunteers who are already there easier to find.

🏘️

Housing associations

Helping residents who want to keep living independently for longer with everyday things.

Two ways to join

Messages and offers

People choose for themselves which organisations they want to follow. You can then send them a message — “The coffee morning has moved to Thursday” — or make an offer, such as a ride. Babbl passes it on when the user is ready for it.

  • Only verified organisations
  • Only with people who chose this themselves
  • You only see what the user shares with you — never conversations

Helping out in a household

If your organisation helps someone regularly, that person can add your staff to their own circle. For example, to see an appointment or pick up a request for help.

  • Only if the user wants it
  • Each staff member is added in the user's home, out loud, and is listed by name
  • The user can remove a single staff member or the whole organisation at any time
Principles

How we work together

✋

The user decides

Organisations only get to see or say anything if the user gives permission for it, organisation by organisation.

⚖️

No paid priority

Nobody can pay to be mentioned by Babbl more often or sooner. Babbl suggests what fits; the user chooses.

🪪

Verified

Only organisations we know to be who they say they are can reach people through Babbl.

🔒

Never conversations

An organisation sees what the user shares, never what someone says to Babbl.

🌱

Wellbeing, not care

Babbl helps with everyday things. It doesn't diagnose anything and doesn't give medical advice.

🛠️

Building together

We develop the integrations with the organisations that will use them, so they fit the way you already work.
